## Hard constraints for this plan

1. **This plan modifies exactly one file.** `app/src/components/OverviewTab.jsx` stays on disk, still imported, still routed, still rendering. Deleting it or touching the nav is Plan 06-02's job, and doing it here would leave a broken intermediate tree.
2. **Chart markup and chart computations are a verbatim port** (D-01, 06-UI-SPEC.md "Hard constraint honored"). The chart wrapper components, the bucketing loops, the numbers they produce, and their empty-state strings all carry over as-is. Nothing in this plan authorises restyling a chart, changing a bucket boundary, or rewording a chart's empty-state line.
3. **The caught-up empty state must not swallow the charts** (06-UI-SPEC.md Interaction Contract — explicitly load-bearing, not discretionary). A user whose attention lists are all empty must still see their funnel, network and activity charts. This is the single most important behavioural requirement in this plan.
4. **Do not widen the meaning of the caught-up condition.** It stays exactly what it is today: a statement about attention items. It must not grow a "…and no chartable data" clause. Those are two independent concepts.
5. **Only three of the retired destination's five content blocks are ported** (06-RESEARCH.md Assumption A1). The four-tile stat-card row and its local tile helper are dropped — Today's own sections already carry that information. The mini force-graph preview card and its graph deep-link callback prop are dropped too — the Network destination's own Graph view is one nav click away and survives this phase. Do not port either, and do not invent a replacement for either.
6. **Do not import symbols the ported code does not use.** `06-PATTERNS.md`'s suggested import list is over-broad: it names two stage-bucket constant arrays that exist only to serve the dropped stat-card row. The ported code needs exactly one new symbol from `../shared.jsx`.
7. **Typography follows the locked contract, not the source file's markup.** The three chart sub-headings demote from the source's section-level heading to the locked 12px/600 Label role, because they now sit *under* a `Section` heading rather than being top-level headings themselves (`06-UI-SPEC.md` Typography). This is the one deliberate styling deviation from a verbatim port, and it is confined to the three `<h3>` class strings.
8. **No mono-typography rollout here.** VIS-02's mono pass covered four specific files in Phase 1; these charts were not among them and belong to Phase 7's sweep. Port the numeric spans with their existing classes.

## Task ordering rationale

Task 1 adds the helper, the imports and the whole `ActivitySection` component without rendering it anywhere — the tree builds, behaviour is unchanged, and the ported computation can be reviewed in isolation. Task 2 does the render-shape surgery, which is small but is the part that carries the real behavioural risk (constraint 3).
