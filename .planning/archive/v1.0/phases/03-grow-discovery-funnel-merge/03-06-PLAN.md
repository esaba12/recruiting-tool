Create the new `app/src/components/GrowTab.jsx` — the page component that makes Company targeting, referral-gap analysis, and people discovery read as one connected flow (GROW-01). It renders exactly 3 always-present `Section`-wrapped children in fixed order — Companies (`ExploreTab`), Coverage (`ReferralCoverageTab`), People (`DiscoverTab`) — per D-01, each with a Mono step-index badge (`step="01"`/`"02"`/`"03"`) per 03-UI-SPEC.md.

`GrowTab` owns two independent pieces of local state, `coverageFocus` and `peopleFocus` (both shaped `{ company, ts: Date.now() }`, RESEARCH.md Pattern 3), plus a ref on each of the Coverage and People `Section` wrapper `<div>`s (RESEARCH.md Pattern 5). Two handlers implement D-04's cross-section linking: `goToCoverage(company)` fires the Coverage section's `scrollIntoView` synchronously, then sets `coverageFocus` (triggered by `ExploreTab`'s new `onTargetAdded` callback, Plan 03-03); `goToPeople(company)` fires the People section's `scrollIntoView` synchronously, then sets `peopleFocus` (triggered by both `ReferralCoverageTab`'s existing `onFindPeople` callback, AND — per the resolved Open Question 1 — `ExploreTab`'s already-added-state "Find people →" button, which continues to jump straight to People without passing through Coverage first).

`initialPeopleFocus` is accepted as a prop (default `null`) and used to seed `peopleFocus`'s initial `useState` value — this is what makes D-05's external deep-link entry point (Pipeline's and Today's "who could I meet here" panels, re-pointed in Plan 03-07) land directly on a pre-focused People section on a fresh `GrowTab` mount.

Purpose: this is the single new file that actually satisfies GROW-01's "one connected flow ... without leaving the destination" success criterion — everything in Wave 1 and Wave 2 was prerequisite infrastructure for this file to exist cleanly.
Output: `app/src/components/GrowTab.jsx` (new).
